Original AI-assisted template construction, not customer observations or independently human-authored judgments. License: CC BY4.0 for these original fixtures. Generator: the unchanged frozen `study/fixtures.py`; contract and SQLite reference are separately implemented algorithms in the same research codebase, not independent human validation.

Main IDs are disjoint from pilot and historical datasets. These families were used in previous development; the new evaluation holds out world instances from the latest pilot, not all prior knowledge of the task families. Each main family has11 worlds. Eight conditions per world yield132 positive and132 withholding cases. The target catalog has acceptable alternative research actions; its constraints are public inputs, and the expected answer set is held outside reader requests.

Records distinguish seller strategy, external account observations and weak feedback. They include valid/observed times, authority, access scope and explicit supersession. A common current-view filter runs before every method. This controls visibility but cannot show superior update maintenance, extraction or native authorization enforcement by one memory. Approved policy changes, corrections, conflicts, scope restrictions and objective changes generate diagnostic snapshots. Distractors and redundant facts expose selection pressure at small budgets.

`inputs.json` contains source events, queries and shared catalogs. `labels.json` is evaluator-only. Manifests provide exact hashes. Scorers check formal action eligibility and supplied provenance, not an objectively best marketing strategy. Mock dry-runs check declared capabilities and prerequisites, not provider effectiveness or revenue.

The independent statistical unit is a generated company world within a fixed family mix. Cases within worlds and repeated relation templates are correlated. Bootstrap intervals describe generator-conditioned comparisons, not generalization to real businesses. Main sample size follows a prospective four-world pilot variance rule with an acknowledged imprecise variance estimate. No real companies, people, emails or private production source are required.

Externally authored messy company documents are a separate exploratory development track under `data/external/messy-company-v1`; they are not pooled into the33-world study and retain upstream MIT licensing.
