\section{Conclusion}
On a small formal B2B marketing task, prerequisite-aware selection retrieves the evidence required for action under a constrained context allowance. The same module composes with the tested general-memory retrieval component, and larger inputs remove the observed support gap. Separately, the shared reader often fails to turn sufficient evidence into a valid decision; its original primary comparison remains uncertain. The contribution is an auditable fixture suite, actual implementation comparisons, retained model evidence and a boundary between context provision and recommendation. It supports testing a domain-specific selection layer and explicit eligibility checks, while leaving raw-document memory quality and strategic next-action usefulness to separate studies.
