\section{Related work and scope of the review}
The review used primary papers and author-maintained artifacts available by 22 October 2026. Ten first-page arXiv searches spanning agent memory, domain specialization, temporal knowledge, recommendation, marketing, sales, evaluation and classical foundations yielded 399 distinct records. Automated title triage, targeted citation tracing and deeper reading of the closest papers are logged separately. These are bounded searches, not an exhaustive systematic review; many candidates and appendices remain unread. Canonical bibliographic metadata and source hashes are retained in the companion evidence, with review depth recorded independently of inclusion in the bibliography.

\paragraph{Marketing and recommendation already use memory.}
RAMP combines client-specific semantic and episodic memory with marketing planning, executable audience filters, verification and reflection~\citep{p250811120}. Its joint planner/memory intervention and small human study address a richer audience-creation workflow than ours; they do not isolate memory representation under a matched total budget. Its repair behavior can relax audience criteria, whereas our contract treats approved constraints as fixed until an explicit revision. MARS connects hierarchical belief-state memory to recommendation~\citep{p260514401}. This directly rules out novelty claims for evolving company knowledge leading to recommendations. We study explicit support and eligibility, not a learned recommendation utility or calibrated belief confidence.

\paragraph{Domain memory is a task-dependent question.}
IFCMemoryBench connects prior conversations to live structured building-model queries~\citep{p260726072}. Its separation of ingestion, retrieval and utilization, and its sensitivity to the probe agent, motivate our common-reader boundary. AFTER evaluates procedural memory across enterprise tasks and roles~\citep{p260623127}; reusable procedures and transferable knowledge should not be assumed equivalent. SalesResearch and CRMArena-Pro evaluate sales-oriented agent work~\citep{p260217017,p250518878}, but different task interfaces, backbones and simulation assumptions prevent using their scores as a memory-only baseline for this study. Our fictional catalogs measure neither those systems' capabilities nor production sales performance.

\paragraph{Temporal evidence and maintenance have close precedents.}
LongMemEval and its later evaluation work make temporal reasoning, updates, abstention and contextual baselines central~\citep{p241010813,p260512493}. Zep uses temporal graph memory~\citep{p250113956}; Hindsight separates factual, experiential and belief-oriented operations~\citep{p251212818}. RECON examines consequences of changed evidence~\citep{p260716716}; AkasicMEM proposes lineage and authorization continuity~\citep{p260925563}. Most directly, EviGraph connects temporal evidence chains to supported decisions, and MemDerive tests derived-state correctness and maintenance~\citep{p261000212,p261025373}. We do not claim to introduce temporal provenance, proof-supported recommendation or dependency tracking. Our simple eligibility view is a shared experimental boundary, not a replacement for these complete systems or a security proof.

\paragraph{Strong simple retrieval changes the comparison.}
MemCompile emphasizes complementary evidence packets rather than independently relevant passages~\citep{p261023313}. Controlled comparisons of memory systems with raw-history retrieval show why reader strength, retriever strength and context allowances must be separated~\citep{p261024671}. ReFind offers another relevant retrieval-oriented comparison~\citep{p260812888}. These works motivate lexical--dense fusion, a generic relation-diversity control, explicit input-token measurement and full context. Standard BM25 and reciprocal-rank fusion have established foundations~\citep{robertson2009,cormack2009}; using them here is implementation reuse of standard methods, not an algorithmic contribution. We do not benchmark deployed Hindsight, Graphiti or other full ingestion systems, because doing so would introduce additional extraction and maintenance differences. Conclusions are restricted to the implemented adapters.

MemoryArena explicitly evaluates interdependent multi-session actions and memory updates from environment feedback~\citep{p260216313}. Its verified ICML 2026 proceedings and controlled task construction make the memory-to-action overlap clear. Our reader is stateless between queries and receives already verified records; successful proposals here cannot establish the closed-loop capabilities evaluated there.

Mem0 evaluates extracted and maintained conversational facts, with a graph variant and a full-context reference~\citep{p250419413}. Its graph and base configurations have different retrieved-token budgets, and full context retains the highest overall judge score in the reviewed paper; such results motivate conditional quality/cost comparisons rather than an assumed graph advantage. Anti-Persistence examines a complementary risk: inferred guidance can influence later trajectories and abstractions~\citep{p261027406}. Its qualified observational analysis does not prove inevitable error growth, but it reinforces the distinction between observed records and inferred policy. Our static-history experiment cannot test that learning loop.

The surviving distinction is consequently modest: an auditable B2B marketing research-action contract, a shared verified-event boundary, and a paired comparison separating domain instructions, generic evidence diversity and public-catalog prerequisite coverage. The study can reveal an engineering boundary or a negative result without establishing state-of-the-art agent memory.
