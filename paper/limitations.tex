\section{Limits of the evidence}
\label{sec:limitations}
The experiment deliberately makes eligibility auditable, which also limits what its results mean. Its rules determine whether a research proposal has the required support. They do not establish that the rules capture a good commercial strategy, that one eligible action is preferable to another, or that a signal predicts purchase intent. The two principal alternatives use the same prerequisites and differ mainly in workflow length. This favors a simple union of required relations; catalogs with heterogeneous alternatives could exceed the budget even when one alternative's support fits. The changed-objective task adds a simpler qualification check. This is a small selection problem, not open-ended campaign planning. A successful mock execution checks a fixed sequence against fictional providers; it measures neither provider reliability nor an outreach outcome. Because execution follows the same contract checks, its completion is largely a consistency diagnostic rather than independent evidence of usefulness.

\paragraph{Clean records and shared safeguards.}
Every adapter receives typed, verified facts and the same time/access filter. The experiment excludes extraction errors, entity resolution, approval workflows, storage migration and the labor of maintaining an ontology. These costs could outweigh a retrieval advantage. Shared filtering and the constrained response grammar remove several opportunities for invalid access and invented identifiers. Their success is an implementation property, not evidence that one memory adapter learned authorization or is secure against adversarial content. Explicit supersession covers only the event semantics defined here; it is not general belief revision. The reader makes a fresh decision for each query and does not update a running action or learn from experience.

\paragraph{Construction and external validity.}
The same AI-assisted research process authored the fixture generator, methods and scorer. Separately implemented Python and SQL checks detect software disagreements but cannot supply independent expert judgment about GTM realism. All test worlds come from one generator and three fixed contract families; held-out families still share policy relations and a common schema with development. Distractors deliberately repeat account/objective vocabulary without supplying required relations, stressing relevance versus complementary coverage; their frequency is not estimated from business documents. Pain and proof are categorical fixture relations, without an assessment of the strength of actual customer evidence. Randomized names, times and identifiers reduce trivial cues without creating independent business histories. The world bootstrap describes variation among these limited generated realizations, not a population of real companies. Distractor levels form a balanced stress grid, so the intervals should be read as resampling summaries, not a calibrated guarantee over arbitrary future histories. Neither the 96 queries nor repeated methods increase the count of independent worlds beyond 12.

\paragraph{Reader, budgets and tuning.}
The study uses one quantized, non-thinking reader on CPU, one embedding model and a compact prerequisite-binding interface. The 1,024- and 1,536-token allowances deliberately stress selection in relatively small records. A stronger reader, another serialization, longer inputs, tool-assisted verification or unstructured documents could change the ranking. Full context is a useful reference within these sizes, not a scalable treatment of arbitrary company history. The retained development sequence includes changes to readers, citation bindings and instructions; this effort was shared across methods before the main freeze, but it remains researcher choice. The study does not establish superiority to complete deployed memory products.

\paragraph{Measurement and reproducibility.}
The primary outcome combines positive support with correct withholding. A method can therefore gain accuracy by recognizing negative cases while still failing to serve positive ones; coverage and its components must accompany the aggregate. The free-text explanation of missing evidence is not semantically scored. No human assessment of strategic usefulness, calibrated confidence, revenue, retention or network effects is available. Identical-request reuse makes the main experiment conditional on one observed response per serialized request. Saved-output reproduction checks the reported analysis; fresh inference may vary with hardware, runtime or model availability. We measure tokens and elapsed time, not infrastructure dollars or energy. Other research and document-preparation processes shared the host, so latency is a descriptive deployment measurement rather than an isolated hardware benchmark. These limits preclude a claim of measured total economic advantage.
