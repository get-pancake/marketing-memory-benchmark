\section{What the evidence supports}
The engineering implication is concrete: preserve enough distinct, authoritative evidence to satisfy an action's prerequisites before asking a model to decide. With competent readers and bounded context, that changes usable recommendation coverage. It can be implemented as a small selection layer over a general memory system; the successful GBrain+coverage control makes replacement of the storage backend unnecessary in this experiment.

The useful specialization is also conditional. Catalog priorities matter under the tightest retrieval allowance, but ordinary relation diversity matches them at 2,048 bytes, and more context erases the remaining retrieval gap. Full context is a serious option for a small company knowledge base. On messy external prose, compilation loses a dated relation and can preserve conflicting claims in a form that one reader overinterprets as unresolved. A stronger chunked baseline eliminates an apparent retrieval defect. A specialized schema is therefore an engineering hypothesis to evaluate, not a universal performance guarantee.

For product design, the evidence favors versioned sources, explicit authority and scope, complete prerequisite coverage, and visible requests for missing evidence. It supports benchmarking both the memory output and each downstream reader. It does not support a claim of a proprietary graph advantage, a learned revenue policy or a network effect. Accumulated company context may become valuable through maintained, reusable evidence; customer retention and economic defensibility require separate evidence.

\paragraph{Limits of inference.}
The main task is small, synthetic and rule-specified. It assumes verified events and a shared current-view filter, uses known relation families and a four-action catalog, and reaches a ceiling with strong readers. It does not evaluate raw extraction, independent update maintenance, native ACLs, large heterogeneous company memory, adversarial instructions or open-ended strategic creativity. One response per treatment estimates behavior at the recorded settings, not model-wide reliability. Bootstrap intervals condition on generated worlds and a fixed family mix. Byte equality is not tokenizer equality or full lifecycle cost equality. API model aliases may change; exact returned identifiers and traces are retained.

The external corpus reduces same-project authorship but remains AI-authored development material from one fictional seller. There is no blinded human usefulness study or independent external reproduction. Qualitative inspection is performed by the same AI-assisted research process and explicitly labeled. The compiled wiki, native search and structured files each exercise only part of a complete memory product. All failed attempts and post-hoc controls are disclosed. Ready-to-release code and a compiled report are separate from public distribution, peer review or paper submission.

\section{Conclusion}
At matched evidence allowances, better coverage can improve grounded marketing action recommendations without changing GPT or Claude. In the controlled study, the gain over general hybrid retrieval is 15.5 percentage points for both readers. Strong generic coverage and a composable GBrain extension match the specialized system at the primary budget; tighter retrieval budgets reveal where domain priorities add value. The practical choice is to measure and maintain action-relevant evidence coverage, while testing the downstream reader and retaining simple full-context and file-memory alternatives.
