# Literature search and review provenance

Cutoff: 22 October 2026 UTC. This is a bounded critical review, not exhaustive coverage or a registered systematic review. Primary sources and author-maintained artifacts support technical statements. Abstract screening, metadata verification, methods/results reading and reproduction are distinct states.

Ten arXiv searches, each limited to its first page of at most 50 results, covered: agent memory evaluation; domain-specific agent memory; temporal knowledge-graph memory; memory/planning/recommendation; marketing-agent memory; agent-memory benchmarks; truth maintenance/case-based reasoning; retrieval-augmented generation/long context; sales-agent benchmarks; and knowledge-based recommendation. They returned 423 occurrences and 399 distinct records. Automated title-keyword triage identified candidates, not independent human inclusion/exclusion decisions. First-page and recency bias remain limitations.

The source set was extended through handoff seeds, backward references and newer relevant studies. The deeper comparison covers RAMP, IFCMemoryBench, MARS, Hindsight, Zep, LongMemEval/V2, EviGraph, MemCompile, ReFind, controlled memory/raw-history comparisons, RECON and AkasicMEM. Pre-results follow-ups expanded MemoryArena, Mem0 and Anti-Persistence to the main-text and appendix depths recorded below and in their individual notes. Selected AFTER, MemDerive, CRM and sales sections were read. The survey taxonomy and GraphRAG introduction received narrower review. Model cards and relevant architecture/pooling sections were checked for the actual runtime. Not every appendix or candidate paper was read.

Canonical metadata for 28 arXiv sources and four DOI sources was verified against primary pages/registry records. The BibTeX file pins the consulted arXiv versions; the audit links exact sources and source-page hashes. Classical case-based reasoning, truth-maintenance and retrieval references were checked bibliographically; most classical full texts remain a review/access gap. Citation tracing was targeted, not an exhaustive forward-citation census. No author results are claimed as independently reproduced by this study.

During frozen main inference, before inspecting its quality outcomes, targeted reading expanded MemoryArena to the full main text and selected construction/runtime/diagnostic appendices. Its ICML 2026 publication was independently verified against the owning PMLR record; main methods/experiments and implementation sections match the consulted arXiv version. The bibliography uses the verified proceedings title (without the arXiv metadata's “MemoryArena:” prefix). See `literature/reviews/memoryarena.md` and `sources/verified-venue-metadata.json`. This follow-up did not change the frozen experiment or turn the review into exhaustive coverage.

Code/artifact work included pinned README/license checks for EnterpriseMem-Bench, LongMemEval/V2, Hindsight, Graphiti and GraphRAG; inspection and execution of selected EnterpriseMem scorer behavior; MemCompile scheduler inspection; and the controlled-memory comparison repository/vendor manifest. The relevant lesson was to isolate reader, information and budget differences, preserve failures, include a strong simple retrieval control, and separate support coverage from decisions. Artifact access gaps are not evidence that upstream code does not exist. Unlicensed implementation code was not copied into this package.

Prior work already connects marketing memory to planning, domain knowledge to tools, evolving beliefs to recommendation, temporal evidence to supported decisions, and complementary retrieval to a shared reader. The contribution is therefore a narrow GTM contract comparison and reproducible fixture suite. The review does not justify a first-ever or state-of-the-art claim. The research assistant authored the review and implementation; no independent human peer review or expert utility assessment is asserted.

A second pre-results follow-up expanded Mem0 to its full main paper and appendices, and More Memory, Worse Agents to the main paper and selected method/analysis/limitations appendices. Current Mem0 and its benchmark owner READMEs/licenses were pinned; current software and hosted claims were kept separate from the historical paper. These deeper reviews are documented in `literature/reviews/mem0.md` and `anti-persistence.md`; no additional model reproduction is claimed.
