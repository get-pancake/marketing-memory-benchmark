# Version 1.0.0 — first public companion

Prepared for public release with **Domain-Specialized Company Memory for Grounded Marketing Recommendations**, by **Guillaume Marquis**.

- Complete code, fictional data, 4,320 main GPT/Opus responses, source hashes and analysis for 120 company worlds and 360 multi-account cases.
- General, marketing-specialized, structured-Markdown, native GBrain-search and full-context methods, plus the audited identical-input generic query-word control.
- Pilot amendments, failures, external wiki extension, cost accounting and historical studies kept separately.
- Final 13-page PDF, editable LaTeX, verified bibliography, eight vector figures and investor note.
- MIT code, CC BY 4.0 original research data/documentation, explicit upstream credits, machine-readable citation and offline CI configuration.

This public packaging updates the title, author and presentation of the internal v4 study. It adds no new model observations or experimental claims. Full saved-output reproduction is tied to the exact manifest and receipt in `release/reproduction-records/clean-reproduction.json`; fresh paid inference remains a separate operation.

The central limitation is explicit: a generic query-word selector reproduces all specialized inputs, wider generic contexts close the gap, and the external general wiki outperforms its marketing counterpart on the small development set. The release is an invitation to test context-selection mechanisms, not a universal vertical-memory or revenue claim.
