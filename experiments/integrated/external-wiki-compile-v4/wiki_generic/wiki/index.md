## Scope and provenance

This wiki compiles supplied records dated November 2024–September 2026, plus undated notes. Source references `[Dxxx]` identify provenance. Contract drafting, signature, and effective dates are kept distinct. Executed instruments, unsigned proposals, CRM claims, meeting recollections, and operational observations remain separately identified; later dates are not treated as automatic corrections.

Pemberline Systems supplies **Pemberline Dock**, yard and dock scheduling software. Contract descriptions include dispatcher consoles, gatehouse kiosks, carrier appointment portals, yard tracking/spotter tasking, and APIs. Those descriptions and commercial permissions are instrument-specific. [D024][D027][D028]
